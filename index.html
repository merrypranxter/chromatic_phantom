CHROMATIC PHANTOM

OPAL MITOSIS
Drag to sculpt the living forms. In Resonance mode, touch to excite the membrane. No glitches, only opalescent weirdness.

MATHEMATICAL ANATOMY

Three genuinely different geometry generators. Mitosis uses a smooth union of evolving signed-distance forms. Resonance uses angular harmonic modes. Pressure Garden moves discrete soft cells with mutual forces. These are artistic models, not calibrated fluids.

CHROMATIC PHANTOM MATERIAL

Soft halos, chromatic contours, surface normals and bright milky highlights respond to the mathematical shape. Approximate optical art, not physically accurate volumetric scattering.

CHROMATIC CHOREOGRAPHY

Built-in colors are starting specimens, not a claim that all your palettes have been imported. Custom arrays are stored with saved artwork.

TIME & DIRECT MANIPULATION

Touch changes actual geometric state in Mitosis and Pressure Garden. In Resonance, touching excites a traveling disturbance. Geometry and animated colors share the same pause clock.

EXPERIMENTS, SAVES & CAPTURE

PNG is implemented. Animation export (high-quality GIF and real H.264 MP4) is a subsequent milestone. Stateful systems do not automatically make seamless loops; we'll validate closure instead of hiding cuts.
